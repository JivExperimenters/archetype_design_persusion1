## Research used in the lessons

| Source | Why it is here | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Sajjacholapunt & Ball, 2014](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00166/full) | Original banner-ad eye-tracking experiment | Does not establish a universal hero rule |
| [Palcu, Sudkamp & Florack, 2017](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.00881/full) | Gaze, attention, and product judgments | Purchase intention is not measured sales |
| [MoMA: Musica Viva, 1957](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/4885) | Historical object record | Check licensing before reproduction |
| [The Met: Carlton, 1981](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/486989) | Historical Memphis object record | Furniture-to-hero adaptation is our interpretation |
| [V&A: What is Postmodernism?](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/what-is-postmodernism) | Institutional historical context | Interpretation differs from original evidence |
| [MoMA: Bauhaus graphic design](https://production-gcp.moma.org/explore/inside_out/2009/11/06/bauhaus-the-graphic-design-department-goes-back-to-school/) | Institutional discussion of grid and typography | Not a universal modernist formula |
| [Cialdini's official overview](https://www.influenceatwork.com/7-principles-of-persuasion/) | Persuasion framework | Tutorial copy is our own application |
| [W3C: text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum) | Readability, including images of text | Contrast alone does not establish accessibility |

For the archetype framework, see Margaret Mark and Carol S. Pearson, *The Hero and the Outlaw* (McGraw-Hill, 2001). Tutorial pairings are creative interpretations, not personality diagnoses or proven performance rankings.

## Generated assets

Four FIRST DETOUR heroes were generated on September 29, 2026 with the **built-in image_gen tool** for this repository:

- [Modernist / photographic](../assets/tutorial/modern-photo.png)
- [Modernist / illustrated](../assets/tutorial/modern-cartoon.png)
- [Postmodernist / photographic](../assets/tutorial/postmodern-photo.png)
- [Postmodernist / illustrated](../assets/tutorial/postmodern-cartoon.png)

The brand, people, scenes, and offer are fictional. Photographic appearance does not make an image a record of a real event. These are teaching mockups, not historical sources or performance evidence.

[Exact prompts](../assets/tutorial/prompts.json) are saved for inspection. These were separate generations; minor differences beyond medium and style remain. Read the gallery critiques before adapting them.

The anatomy, gaze, and responsive-layout SVGs are original instructional schematics. Their arrows represent intended relationships, not eye-tracking data. Museum images are linked, not copied.
