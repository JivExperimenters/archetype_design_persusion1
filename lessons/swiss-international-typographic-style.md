## What it is

Swiss / International Typographic Style developed in Switzerland during the 1950s. Designers focused on clarity, organization, and communication rather than decoration. The style became known for clean layouts, sans-serif typefaces, grids, and strong visual hierarchy.

It is closely connected to Modernism because it emphasizes function, simplicity, and logical structure. Instead of adding decoration, Swiss designers used typography, spacing, images, and grids to communicate information clearly.

## Recognize and apply it

<div style="display:grid; grid-template-columns:repeat(3, minmax(0, 1fr)); gap:14px; margin:20px 0;">
  <div style="background:#d72638; border:4px solid #111111; padding:16px; color:#ffffff;">
    <h3 style="margin:0 0 10px; font-size:1.2rem;">Grid-based layouts</h3>
    <p style="margin:0; line-height:1.5;">Information is organized using columns and consistent margins to create rhythm and order.</p>
  </div>
  <div style="background:#f3d000; border:4px solid #111111; padding:16px; color:#111111;">
    <h3 style="margin:0 0 10px; font-size:1.2rem;">Sans-serif typography</h3>
    <p style="margin:0; line-height:1.5;">Clean, neutral typefaces make the message feel modern, direct, and easy to read.</p>
  </div>
  <div style="background:#0f4c81; border:4px solid #111111; padding:16px; color:#ffffff;">
    <h3 style="margin:0 0 10px; font-size:1.2rem;">Asymmetrical hierarchy</h3>
    <p style="margin:0; line-height:1.5;">The layout feels balanced, but the eye is guided by contrast in scale, weight, and spacing.</p>
  </div>
</div>

This style is useful when I want a design to feel precise, modern, and highly readable. For a hero section, I could use a disciplined grid, bold sans-serif typography, and one strong image with plenty of empty space to keep the message clear.
