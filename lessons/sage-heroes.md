# Sage — Four-Hero Package
[Home](../README.md)

## Brand concept

**Brand:** ClearMind  
**Product:** An online learning platform  
**Audience:** Curious people who want reliable knowledge without the noise  
**Core offer:** Clear, structured lessons that turn complex ideas into understandable insight

The same brand, audience, product, and core offer are used across all four heroes.

---

## Hero 1 — Swiss / International Typographic Style + Authority

### Hero

**Headline:** Know what matters.

**Supporting copy:** ClearMind strips away the noise and gives you expert-led lessons that make difficult ideas feel clear, practical, and trustworthy.

**CTA:** Explore the lessons

**Visual direction:** Use a Swiss-style grid, clean sans-serif typography, strong hierarchy, generous spacing, and a structured layout built to signal clarity and authority.

### Explanation

The Sage archetype is expressed through the desire for understanding, expertise, and reliable knowledge. Authority is communicated by presenting expert learning as the route to credibility and clarity. The Swiss visual system supports that message through disciplined structure, calm spacing, and strong information hierarchy.

**Adapted source feature:** Grid-based organization and precise typography adapted from Swiss / International Typographic Style.

---

## Hero 2 — Deconstruction + Authority

### Hero

**Headline:** Question the signal.

**Supporting copy:** Learn from expert perspectives that break complex ideas apart, compare them, and help you understand what actually holds up.

**CTA:** Start exploring

**Visual direction:** Use overlapping text and imagery, asymmetrical alignment, fragmented layers, and experimental composition inspired by deconstruction.

### Explanation

The Sage archetype appears through investigation, intellectual rigor, and the urge to understand ideas beyond the surface. Authority is reinforced by presenting expert knowledge as a foundation for evaluating arguments critically. The deconstructive layout mirrors the act of breaking down assumptions and seeing relationships more clearly.

**Adapted source feature:** Layered text, overlapped forms, and unconventional organization adapted from deconstructive design.

## Hero 3 — Swiss / International Typographic Style + Social Proof

### Hero

**Headline:** Learn alongside people who think deeper.

**Supporting copy:** Join a growing community of curious learners using ClearMind to explore new subjects, sharpen their understanding, and build real knowledge.

**CTA:** Start learning

**Visual direction:** Use a clean grid, restrained color, crisp sans-serif typography, and high legibility to create a calm, credible, community-driven learning aesthetic.

### Explanation

The Sage archetype appears through the value of learning, inquiry, and intellectual growth. Social Proof is communicated by showing that others are already learning with ClearMind and finding value in organized, trustworthy information. The Swiss style keeps the experience confident and readable, reinforcing the impression that this is a serious place to learn.

**Adapted source feature:** Structured grid and typographic hierarchy adapted from Swiss / International Typographic Style.

## Hero 4 — Deconstruction + Social Proof

### Hero

**Headline:** See the pattern. Change the way you think.

**Supporting copy:** Discover how other curious learners use ClearMind to challenge assumptions, explore difficult ideas, and reconnect with what they really understand.

**CTA:** Discover ClearMind

**Visual direction:** Use overlapping imagery, fragmented layouts, unusual type relationships, and layered visual elements inspired by deconstruction to suggest questioning and discovery.

### Explanation

The Sage archetype is visible in curiosity, challenge, and the desire to understand ideas from different angles. Social Proof is communicated through a sense that other learners are already engaging deeply with the platform and its ideas. The deconstructive composition pushes the hero away from ordinary layouts and creates a feeling of investigation and re-framing.

**Adapted source feature:** Overlapping elements, fragmented composition, and unexpected visual rhythms adapted from deconstructive design.

---

## Prompt

Create four advertising hero concepts for ClearMind, an online learning platform. Keep the same brand, product, audience, core offer, and image dimensions across all four concepts. Use a clear headline, short supporting copy, and a visible CTA. For the Swiss versions, prioritize clarity, hierarchy, and measured structure. For the deconstruction versions, use overlap, fragmentation, and experimental composition to suggest inquiry and insight. The overall message should communicate expertise, curiosity, understanding, and trust.

---

## Rejected draft

**Rejected headline:** “We know everything.”

**Why it was rejected:** The claim feels inflated and makes a broad promise that the brand cannot credibly stand behind. It focuses on ego rather than helpful expertise.

**Revision:** “Know what matters.”

The revised version keeps the authority angle while sounding grounded, credible, and useful to the audience.

---

## Strongest hero

Hero 1 is the strongest option because it most clearly balances the Sage archetype with the authority principle. It communicates expertise without sounding exaggerated, and the Swiss-style structure makes the trust signal immediate and readable. The deconstruction variants feel more exploratory and expressive, but they are less direct for a core learning message that needs credibility first.
