## My archetype and design choices

Chosen archetype: The Sage.

AI's suggestion and whether I agree (with a reason): AI suggested that I am a Sage, and I agreed. The Sage values wisdom, curiosity, and understanding. This fits me because I like to know why something works instead of just accepting an answer. When making a decision, I prefer to ask questions, compare different viewpoints, and think through the reasoning behind each option. I also appreciate clear explanations that make complicated ideas easier to understand. For my personal page, the Sage archetype gives me a direction that feels thoughtful, approachable, and focused on sharing useful information.

Imagery, color, and type cues: Use images of books, libraries, research, and simple diagrams to emphasize knowledge and discovery. A palette of deep navy, white, and muted gray can create a calm, focused appearance. Pair classic serif headings with readable sans-serif body text, using generous spacing to make information easy to follow.

Modern or postmodern style: A modernist design approach generally fits the Sage because its emphasis on order, simplicity, and function supports clear communication. Structured grids, consistent typography, and restrained decoration help visitors focus on information and understand how ideas connect. A postmodern approach can also suit a Sage that encourages questioning assumptions and exploring multiple perspectives, using unexpected layouts or mixed visual references to invite curiosity. For this design direction, modernism is the stronger fit because it supports readability and a calm, organized presentation of knowledge.

Headline: "Explore Ideas. Build Understanding."

CTA and destination: "Explore the Resources" — leads to a resource page with educational articles, guides, and research organized by topic. This gives visitors a clear next step toward learning more.

Persuasion principle and why it fits: Authority. The Sage builds trust through demonstrated knowledge, clear explanations, and reliable evidence. Citing credible sources and showing relevant expertise supports this principle while helping visitors make informed decisions.
