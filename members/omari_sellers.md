## A personal brand direction

The **Creator** archetype feels like a good fit for me. I enjoy making things, testing ideas, and finding my own way to solve problems. I would rather build and experiment than simply follow an existing formula.

| Design element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Imagery** | Cameras and video gear, cars and roads, technology, sketches, blank canvases, works in progress, and behind-the-scenes process |
| **Color** | Black and white with a bright blue or orange accent |
| **Typography** | A clean sans serif such as Montserrat, Poppins, or Inter |
| **Voice** | “Build it. Learn from it. Keep creating.” |

I could share useful tips about content creation, technology, or starting projects. I could also document goals and progress publicly; showing the process would help make the brand feel personal and demonstrate follow-through.

## What I learned

This project helped me see how typography, layout, color, shapes, and imagery can change the way a message feels and how easily people understand it. I also gained experience using Git and GitHub to organize work, create branches and commits, and collaborate with a team.

## My design approach

I want visual choices to support the message, not distract from it. I start by considering the audience and purpose, then choose typography, layout, imagery, and color that fit the intended communication.
